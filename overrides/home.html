Version 1.0 · desktop app

Your YouTube Music library, as a fast desktop player.

AMP streams each song in small segments and keeps them on disk: playback starts right away, you can seek anywhere, and songs you've played before start instantly. No account needed to search and play.

How it works

The window you see is a Tauri app; a small Python backend runs next to it and stops with it.

1

Find

Search YouTube Music, or open your liked songs, playlists and saved queues.

2

Stream

The backend resolves the audio, reads its index and fetches the segments around the playhead first.

3

Replay

Segments stay on disk (500 MB by default, least recently played go first), so replays start at once.

Show what's playing on stream

Add one Browser source in OBS and AMP keeps it up to date: cover, title, artist and a gently moving wave that freezes when you pause. Size it with ?w= and ?h=; everything scales.
