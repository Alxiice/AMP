Version 1.0 · desktop app

AMP is a desktop player for YouTube Music.

It downloads songs in small pieces and keeps them on your disk, so a song starts playing quickly and you can jump to any point in it. Songs you've already played start right away. You can search and play without an account.

What it does

AMP runs in its own window, with a small backend on your computer doing the downloading.

How it works

The window is a Tauri app. A small Python program runs alongside it and stops when you close AMP.

1

Find

You search YouTube Music, or open your liked songs, playlists or a saved queue.

2

Stream

The backend finds the song's audio and downloads the pieces around where you're listening first.

3

Replay

Pieces stay on disk (up to 500 MB by default, oldest removed first), so playing a song again is instant.

Show what's playing on stream

Add a Browser source in OBS pointing at AMP's widget and it shows the song that's playing: cover, title, artist and progress. It stops moving when you pause. Set its size with ?w= and ?h=.
